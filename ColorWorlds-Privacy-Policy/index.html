1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application ColorWorlds (Android package ID: com.aistudio.colorworlds.puzzle3d), a 3D block puzzle game distributed via the Google Play Store.

We value your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how data is processed when you download, install, and play ColorWorlds on your Android device.

By downloading, installing, or playing ColorWorlds, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the game.

2. Information We Collect

ColorWorlds is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information to enjoy the game.

The game does NOT collect, request, or store:

  • Your real name or physical address
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your contact lists or address book
  • Your device camera or microphone streams
  • Your photos, media files, or external documents
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Payment card details or bank account information
No In-App Purchases: ColorWorlds is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by third-party in-app advertisements. It does not collect financial details or payment credentials.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you run ColorWorlds on your Android device, integrated platform components and third-party software development kits (SDKs)—specifically the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob)—may automatically process technical and diagnostic signals necessary for game stability, ad serving, and fraud detection.

This automated processing occurs in the background during standard gameplay and does not require active input from the player.

4. Device and Technical Information

Integrated third-party services and system libraries may automatically receive non-sensitive technical parameters from your device, including:

  • Device Specifications: Hardware model, manufacturer, screen resolution, screen density, available memory, and graphical capability.
  • Operating System: Android OS version and build level.
  • App Metadata: ColorWorlds version number, package identifier (com.aistudio.colorworlds.puzzle3d), and installation source.
  • Language & Locale: Device system language and general regional settings.
  • Network Information: General connection type (e.g., Wi-Fi or cellular network), carrier info, and Internet Protocol (IP) address.

This information is utilized strictly to ensure device compatibility, maintain rendering performance across different screen sizes, diagnose unexpected crashes, and prevent unauthorized service abuse.

5. Gameplay and Local Progress Data

ColorWorlds stores your level progression, puzzle scores, completed worlds, move counts, and earned star ratings locally on your device using Android Room local database storage.

This progress information remains stored on your local device hardware and is not uploaded to or tracked on proprietary cloud servers. If you clear the application data or uninstall the app from your device, your local puzzle progress may be reset according to standard Android operating system behavior.

6. Advertising in ColorWorlds

To keep ColorWorlds free for all players, we display advertisements within the application provided by Google AdMob.

The active ad formats in ColorWorlds include:

  • Banner Ads: Small rectangular ads displayed at designated top or bottom areas of the screen.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen advertisements displayed at natural puzzle transition points (e.g., after completing a world level).
  • Native Advanced Ads: Clean, styled ad elements integrated directly into the game menu cards and layout.

Note: ColorWorlds does not currently utilize Rewarded Video Ads.

7. Google AdMob Integration

Advertising in ColorWorlds is delivered through Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area).

When ads are served in ColorWorlds, Google AdMob may process technical data, such as:

  • Mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • IP address (used to infer coarse geographic region for contextual relevancy and fraud prevention).
  • Ad interaction metrics (e.g., ad impressions, clicks, view duration).
  • Diagnostic information and technical device attributes.

Google acts as an independent data controller for advertising services. You can learn more about how Google collects and uses information by visiting:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Ads Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

8. Advertising Identifiers

The Google Advertising ID (GAID) is a user-resettable, unique identifier provided by Android OS for advertising purposes.

Advertising partners use this identifier to:

  • Serve contextual or personalized advertisements where permitted by your device settings and local regulations.
  • Implement frequency capping so that players are not shown identical ads repetitively.
  • Detect and prevent invalid clicks, fraud, and bot traffic.
  • Measure ad delivery performance and reporting.

You have full control over your advertising identifier. You can reset or delete it at any time directly through your Android device settings.

9. Analytics and Diagnostic Data

We may review aggregated diagnostic metrics and crash reports provided via the Google Play Console to detect performance regressions, memory leaks, and operating system incompatibilities.

These reports contain anonymized, aggregated system telemetry (e.g., stack traces, Android version, device manufacturer) and do not contain personal identifiers or private user communications.

10. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with ColorWorlds is utilized strictly for lawful, technical, and operational purposes:

  • Gameplay Operation: To deliver 3D puzzle mechanics, animations, sound effects, and level progressions smoothly.
  • Performance Optimization: To optimize battery efficiency, frame rate stability, and responsive touch controls.
  • Ad Serving & Monetization: To display, measure, and manage non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and native advertisements.
  • Bug Fixing & Stability: To detect software anomalies, resolve runtime exceptions, and publish stability updates.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: To safeguard the game and its ad network integrations against invalid traffic, cheating, and malicious activities.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable statutory laws, Google Play Developer Distribution agreements, and regulatory obligations.

11. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information.

Data processed in connection with ColorWorlds is only shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-Party Ad Networks: With Google AdMob to deliver in-game ads, prevent fraudulent impressions, and measure advertising metrics.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • Protection of Rights: When necessary to enforce our legal rights, investigate security violations, or protect the safety of users.

13. Children's Privacy

ColorWorlds is intended for a general audience. The game does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information directly from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in certain European jurisdictions) in violation of applicable laws, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us without parental consent, please contact us immediately at the contact email provided below so that we can take prompt steps to investigate and remove any such data.

14. Data Security

We employ standard administrative, logical, and technical security practices to safeguard data. Puzzle game records are maintained securely on your local device sandbox.

All network communications with third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob) occur over encrypted HTTPS connections utilizing industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Please note that no method of electronic transmission or mobile storage is 100% immune to vulnerabilities, but we continuously implement security measures to protect your experience.

15. Data Retention

Because ColorWorlds does not operate external account databases or cloud user repositories, we do not retain user identity profiles on proprietary servers.

Your gameplay scores and unlocked levels remain on your device until you choose to clear the app's storage or uninstall the application.

Third-party service providers (such as Google) retain operational logs and advertising data in accordance with their respective data retention policies and legal retention mandates.

16. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You have full control over your privacy settings and data processing on your Android device:

A. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can opt out of personalized (interest-based) advertising at any time through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your device Settings app.
  2. Tap Google > All Services > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  3. Select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

Result: After opting out or deleting your advertising ID, you will still see advertisements in the game, but they will be contextual rather than personalized based on your browsing interests.

B. Managing Regional Consent

Where required by local privacy laws (such as in the European Economic Area / UK), consent preference dialogues are presented to allow players to manage their ad consent choices directly.

C. Industry Opt-Out Portals

You may also use industry-wide web opt-out tools:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance: https://optout.aboutads.info
  • Network Advertising Initiative: https://optout.networkadvertising.org
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance: https://www.youronlinechoices.eu

17. Data Deletion and Account Inquiries

Because ColorWorlds does not create or manage user accounts, you can delete all locally stored game data and achievements simply by clearing the application storage via:

Settings > Apps > ColorWorlds > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage

For any privacy-related questions, inquiries, or requests regarding third-party processing, you can contact the developer at the email listed below.
